# The bank demands evidence the LOOPS KB corpus does not contain

**Date:** 2026-09-09 · **Runs:** the 2026-09-08 loops-kb sweeps under `data/runs/`

## Finding

Across all 50 questions in banks v1–v7 there are 60 `expect_citations` entries.
Checked against the documents actually present in the KB corpus (1,740 redacted
`bre/` + `docs/` files) and the excluded raw `source/` tree (2,216 files):

| where the expected document lives | count | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| in the KB corpus | 36 | 60% |
| only in the excluded raw source | 3 | 5% |
| nowhere in the snapshot | 21 | 35% |

**19 of 50 questions require at least one document the KB cannot retrieve.**
The 21 absent names — `UIMB0730`, `UIMB0440`, `DXCD037S`, `UIMV_SSN_RETIRED`,
`UIMT_RSA_NONMON`, `JO7UD158`, `L2ACPD`, `REPRA262`, and others — look like DB2
views, job names and programs from parts of the system outside the CA-7 daily
package, even though every bank declares `corpus: quick_space_ca7_daily_s3`.

## Effect on the scores

| subset | deterministic | blind judge (Opus 5) |
|---|---:|---:|
| all 50 | 18/50 (36%) | 346/500 (69%) |
| 31 answerable from the corpus | **17/31 (55%)** | 223/310 (72%) |
| 19 with an impossible requirement | 1/19 (5%) | 123/190 (65%) |

The 36% headline was not a retrieval score. It was 55% on the questions the
KB could answer, dragged down by 19 questions it could not pass by
construction.

The judge's 65% on the impossible subset is the other half of the story: the
KB answered those questions on topic and honest about gaps while structurally
unable to cite what the key demanded. That is the behaviour that distinguishes
it from a system that fills the gap with a confident guess.

## Why

The reference answers were generated by a model analysing the COBOL and
Transform output — material broader than the CA-7 daily package the KB
indexes. The keys therefore cite documents the corpus never contained. This is
a scoping defect in the bank, not a retrieval defect in the KB, and no amount of
retrieval tuning against this corpus can move it.

Ingesting the excluded raw `source/` tree would repair only 3 of the 24
impossible citations. The remaining 21 are outside the snapshot entirely.

## What has to change

1. **Tag each question with the corpus it assumes.** `corpus:` at the bank
   level is currently a single value for all 50 questions and is wrong for 19.
2. **Disposition each of the 19**: drop the impossible requirement, mark the
   question out of scope for this corpus, or bring the missing source under the
   source matrix with owner approval. Not scoring them against this KB is not an
   option — that is what produced 36%.
3. **Report the answerable subset separately** until the bank is re-scoped.
   55% deterministic / 72% judge on 31 questions is the honest current number,
   and it is still depressed by check-shape defects recorded elsewhere (the
   planted-misspelling requirement on v5-Q2; `UIMB0725`/`UIMB0726` on v3-Q4,
   which exist nowhere).

Reproduce with the script in the effort log for 2026-09-09; inputs are the bank
YAML, the redacted corpus tree, and `scores.csv` from each run.
